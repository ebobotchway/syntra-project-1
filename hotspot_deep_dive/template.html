Who is involved

Blue = a named road-user group. Gray = not a specific group (Unknown, Other road user).

Key findings

Fixed text for all years 2017-2024; it does not change with the filters.

  • Passenger cars (70.2% of all accidents) and bicycles (24.8%) are involved most often. Pedestrians follow at 10.3%, vans 8.1%, mopeds 8.0%, motorcycles 6.8%, trucks 4.4%, buses 1.4%.
  • "Unknown" is 10.7%: for about one accident in ten the road user is not recorded.
  • Shares add up to more than 100% because one accident can involve two groups (136,144 accidents involve two different groups).

Read with care: these are raw counts. A group that is common on the road appears often for that reason alone; severity and traffic volume are not part of this graph.

1b

Annual change

Key findings

Fixed text for all years 2017-2024; it does not change with the filters.

  • Almost every group drops in 2020 (bicycles only -5.5%; buses -37.7% and pedestrians -30.4% most) and rebounds in 2021-2022.
  • By 2024 (2017 = 100): bicycles 115, mopeds 105, vans and buses 97, pedestrians 86, motorcycles 83, passenger cars 82, trucks 73.
  • Post vs pre (average per year 2022-2024 against 2017-2019): trucks -17.2%, motorcycles -12.9%, cars -12.2%, pedestrians -10.4%, buses -6.4%, vans +1.5%, mopeds +7.3%, bicycles +14.6%.
  • "Unknown" more than doubles (+113% from 2017 to 2024). Part of the decline of the named groups may be recording shifting into "Unknown"; the data cannot show that.

Read with care: "Covid-19" is my own label for the periods. These are accident counts, not rates, so fewer accidents could also mean less traffic.

        About and caveats

        • Data: Belgian road-accident open data 2017-2024 (289,532 accidents); residents per municipality and year, and per statistical sector (2023), from Statbel (population on 1 January, CC BY 4.0). Municipality outlines: Statbel statistical sectors 2023, CC BY 4.0.
        • Counting: an accident counts for a group if the group is road user 1 or road user 2, once per accident; "all four" counts an accident with two of them once. Severe = fatal, fatally injured or seriously injured (classes 1-3).
        • Grid: squares of 5 km x 5 km (25 km2), aligned to the Belgian Lambert 1972 grid. Only accidents with usable coordinates are in the squares (89% of all, 85% before Covid-19, 96% after it, fewer in Wallonia).
        • Residents of a square: Statbel's residents per statistical sector (1 January 2023) are spread over the squares by area (people are assumed to be spread evenly inside a sector) and scaled to each year with the growth of the municipality. A rate per square needs 1,000 residents and 30 accidents.
        • Rates: accidents divided by the residents of each year, per 100,000 per year. A rate is shown in full colour from 100 accidents of the group in the period; 30-99 accidents are faded and flagged; fewer stay gray.
        • Periods: pre (2017-2019), during (2020-2021) and post (2022-2024) Covid-19 are my own definition; the data states no cause. Accidents per year make the periods comparable (3, 2 and 3 years).
        • Limits: residents are not traffic or visitors; hotspots are where accidents are counted; associations, not causes.

Hotspots of accidents with vulnerable road users

Before (2017-2019), during (2020-2021) and after (2022-2024) Covid-19: where accidents with pedestrians, cyclists, motorcycles and mopeds concentrate in 5 km2 cells and 5 x 5 km squares, how severe they are there, and how it looks per resident. An accident counts once for every group it involves. These are counts and rates, not causes.

        About and caveats

        • Data: Belgian road-accident open data 2017-2024 (289,532 accidents); residents per municipality and year from Statbel (population on 1 January, CC BY 4.0). Municipality outlines: Statbel statistical sectors 2023, CC BY 4.0.
        • Counting: an accident counts for a group if the group is road user 1 or road user 2, once per accident; "all four" counts an accident with two of them once. Severe = fatal, fatally injured or seriously injured (classes 1-3).
        • Grid: cells of 5 km2 (side 2.24 km) and squares of 5 km x 5 km, aligned to the Belgian Lambert 1972 grid. Only accidents with usable coordinates are in the cells (89% of all, 85% before Covid-19, 96% after it, fewer in Wallonia).
        • Rates: accidents divided by the residents of each year, per 100,000 per year. A rate is shown in full colour from 100 accidents of the group in the period; 30-99 accidents are faded and flagged; fewer stay gray.
        • Periods: pre (2017-2019), during (2020-2021) and post (2022-2024) Covid-19 are my own definition; the data states no cause. Accidents per year make the periods comparable (3, 2 and 3 years).
        • Limits: residents are not traffic or visitors; hotspots are where accidents are counted; associations, not causes.
